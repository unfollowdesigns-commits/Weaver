# Formats

## Ranked list (top N, shortlist)
| # | Candidate | Score | Current role | Strongest signal | Watch item |
Score as "3.9 / 5 · Yes". ★/☆ after the name. One line under the table: who to call first and why.

## Scored candidate (detail)
**Name ★** · 3.9 / 5 · Yes
Two or three sentences: the main reason and the biggest risk.
Where they fit: bullets. Where to probe: bullets.
Criteria: one line each, "Label: 4. Evidence."
Pay (not used in the score): base, bonus, against the search's range, only if on file.

## Call prep (five lines)
1. Who they are now: role, company, scope.
2. Strongest signal.
3. What to probe (two or three).
4. From the notes: availability, location, motivation.
5. One opening question.

## One-page profile (like the firm's report pages)
Name, current title and company, location. Stage. Pay facts if on file (current base, bonus, equity, against the range). Where they fit (four or five bullets with numbers and companies). Where to probe (three or four). Career: years, title, company, newest first. Education and credentials (no years).
