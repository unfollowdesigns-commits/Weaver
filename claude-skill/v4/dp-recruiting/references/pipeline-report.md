# Client pipeline report (the firm's format)

Always a draft for the recruiter to check and edit. Never send anything yourself.

Sections, in order:
1. Cover: "Search pipeline report", the job title, one line: "Where the search stands today, drawn from N leaders across ...", prepared for (client), prepared by District Partners, the date. The funnel counts: Outbound (everyone on the search), Responded and initial conversation, First layer screen, Second layer screen and cleared, Submitted to client; each counts those who reached that stage or beyond.
2. Top 10 candidates, ranked: #, candidate (★/☆), company and location, a two-line assessment, stage. Then "Where we're focusing next" in two sentences.
3. Where the search stands: one paragraph with the counts. A table for everyone at first layer screen or beyond: candidate, status, base, bonus, strongest signal, watch item. Then "How to read the pipeline": two or three short boxes grouping candidates by what they bring and the trade-off (for example industry-led vs function-led profiles), plus "The compensation picture" from the pay facts on file (ranges, above or within the search's range).
4. One profile page per candidate cleared or submitted (format in formats.md).
5. The team's screening scorecard for those candidates, if the team wrote one in Loxo: each criterion with its score and note, the average, the recommendation. Scale line: 1 Does not meet · 2 Weak · 3 Meets · 4 Strong · 5 Exceptional.
6. First layer screen: each candidate with "Signal." and "Watch." lines.
7. Responded and initial conversation: name, company and title, industry tag, location.
8. Outbound: name, company, title, location.
9. No longer in process, if any: name and a placeholder for the reason.

Rules: pay appears only as facts from Loxo and notes. Scores shown to the client come from the team's own scorecards, not from Weaver's or your first read, unless the recruiter asks otherwise. Mark the draft "Draft for review". Produce it as a document the recruiter can edit and export to PDF.
