# Scoring a candidate, Weaver's method

Use this whenever you score. Same method as Weaver, so scores are comparable.

## 1. The scorecard
- Use the search's own scorecard template in Loxo (the job's scorecard template criteria), in its order, with its exact labels.
- No template on the search: draft nine criteria from the job description (industry, two or three on the core of the function, scale or stage, partnership with the CEO or leadership, location if the job sets one, being the face in the market), show them to the user, and score only after they agree.
- For each criterion, decide what a 3 (meets the bar for this role) and a 5 (clearly exceptional for this role) look like before scoring.
- Industry: from the job, say what counts as direct (★) and adjacent (☆) for this search.

## 2. The record
Read: current role and company, work history (titles, companies, dates, descriptions), education (no years), the latest resume text, the team's notes (newest first). Leave out pay and anything in the ground rules. Read the job description too, without its salary.

## 3. Score
- Each criterion: a whole number 1 to 5 from evidence in the record only, with one short sentence of evidence naming where it comes from (a role, the resume, a note).
- No evidence: no score, "No evidence in the record". Never a middle score as a guess. A job title alone is not evidence of what someone did: do not write "likely".
- Average: of the scored criteria only, one decimal. Compute it yourself; do not estimate.
- Recommendation: Yes is the short list, not a pass mark: every must-have met, strong (4 or 5) on most of what matters, an average of 3.5 or more, and evidence on at least seven of nine criteria. Otherwise No. Expect most candidates on a search to be No. A No at 4 or more needs a one-line reason.
- Write "the candidate" or "they", never he or she: gender is unknown and is never guessed. Do not quote the scorecard's rules back; say what the record shows.
- Strongest signal and watch item: one line each, with a fact.
- Where they fit: two to four concrete facts that match the search. Where to probe: one to three points for the first call.
- Flags when it applies: few criteria with evidence, no resume, only part of the record read.

## 4. Present
Format in `formats.md`. Say it is a first read from the Loxo record, before any conversation.
