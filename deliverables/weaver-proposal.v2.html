Under the hood

Exactly what happens to each candidate

For you or anyone reviewing the setup. Every step uses the official connections of Loxo and Anthropic.

1TriggerLoxo tells Weaver the moment a candidate is added to a chosen search. An hourly check catches anything missed.
2Read from LoxoWeaver reads the candidate's profile, work history, latest resume text and notes through Loxo's API, about five requests per candidate. Read-only until you approve the test.
3PrepareName, email, phone, address, pay and graduation years are removed. Long notes or resumes are trimmed, and the scorecard says so.
4Score with ClaudeOne request to Claude per candidate, under your firm's Anthropic account. Claude must answer in a fixed format: a score and a reason per question, a recommendation, open questions.
5CheckWeaver rejects any incomplete or out-of-range answer and asks again once. The average and rank are calculated by Weaver, not guessed by the AI.
6Write to LoxoOne scorecard per candidate, marked as an AI assessment awaiting review. Nothing else in Loxo is changed.
7Your teamReads, agrees or not, decides. Claude connected to Loxo can then answer questions using the same scores.

Security. Access keys live in the hosting provider's encrypted settings, never in documents or email. Anthropic does not train its models on data sent through its business API by default. Any access can be revoked at any time.
