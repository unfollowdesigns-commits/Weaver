# Reading the AI first read in Loxo

One page for recruiters. Replaces v3 (30 Oct 2026): how Yes and No are decided, the below-bar
flag, criteria drafted for every search.

## What it is

Before anyone has spoken to a candidate, Weaver reads their Loxo record and resume
against the search's scorecard. It is a first read to help you decide who to call
first. Your own scorecard after the screens stays yours.

## What you will see

On candidates in searches where it is switched on, a scorecard written by
**"Candidate scoring (AI, awaiting review)"**:

- **4.1 / 5 · Yes**: the average of the scored criteria and the recommendation.
  **Yes** is the short list: no must-have missed for this search, an average of 3.5 or more,
  and evidence on at least 7 of the 9 criteria. **No** means a must-have is missed (for
  example industry experience, or the notes rule out the location), or the record is under
  that bar. The same rule applies on every search.
- **★ / ☆**: direct industry experience, or adjacent.
- **Strongest signal** and **watch item**, one line each.
- **Where they fit** and **where to probe** on the first call.
- **A score per criterion**, 1 Does not meet, 2 Weak, 3 Meets, 4 Strong,
  5 Exceptional, with one line of evidence. No score means the record had no
  evidence for it. That is deliberate.
- **Flags**, when something needs your eye.

The criteria are the search's own: the criteria names from your scorecard in Loxo, with what a
3 and a 5 look like drafted from the job description the first time Weaver sees the search.
The partner on the search can adjust them at any time; everyone is then re-read on the new
version.

## It is advice, not a decision

You decide who moves forward. Agree, disagree or ignore it. When you disagree,
a short note helps us tune it for your team.

## The flags

| Flag | What it means |
|---|---|
| thin_record / partial_record | Little evidence; the score rests on a few points |
| no_resume | No resume file in Loxo; read from the profile, work history and notes |
| resume_truncated / resume_trimmed | Only part of a very long resume was read |
| notes_trimmed | Only the most recent notes were read |
| below_bar | The AI found no missed must-have, but the average or the evidence is under the bar, so it is listed as No. Near misses: worth a look if the reasons fit |
| check: recommended no with an average of 4 or more | A must-have is missed despite strong scores; read which one |
| "The record contains text aimed at screening tools" (in where to probe) | The resume tried to instruct the AI; it was ignored |
| pay_removed | The AI mentioned pay and that line was removed; pay is never part of the score |

## What it never looks at

Names, contact details, photos, graduation years, or anything about age,
gender, ethnicity, religion, disability or family. It refers to every candidate as "the
candidate" or "they". Pay is not used in the score; it is shown to you separately in the
report, from Loxo's pay fields.
