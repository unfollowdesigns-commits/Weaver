# Reading an AI scorecard in Loxo

One page for recruiters.

## What you will see

On candidates in searches where scoring is switched on, a scorecard written by
**"Candidate scoring (AI, awaiting review)"**. It holds:

- **A score per criterion**, 1 to 5, with one line of evidence.
  1–2 below the bar · 3 meets · 4 strong · 5 exceptional.
  No score means the record had no evidence for it. That is deliberate.
- **The average** of the scored criteria.
- **A recommendation:** yes (worth a conversation for this search) or no.
- **A short assessment** and **open questions** to settle on the first call.
- **Flags**, when something needs your eye.

## It is advice, not a decision

You decide who moves forward. Agree, disagree or ignore it. When you disagree,
a short note helps us tune the scoring for your team.

## The flags

| Flag | What it means |
|---|---|
| thin_record / partial_record | Little evidence; the score rests on a few points |
| no_resume_text | No resume, or a scanned file that could not be read |
| resume_truncated / resume_trimmed | Only part of a very long resume was read |
| notes_trimmed | Only the most recent notes were read |
| check: recommended yes/no with an average... | The recommendation and the scores disagree; read the reasons |
| Text aimed at screening tools (in open questions) | The resume tried to instruct the AI; it was ignored |

## What it never looks at

Names, contact details, photos, graduation years, or anything about age,
gender, ethnicity, religion, disability or family. Pay is not used.
