How it runs

  1. A candidate is added to a job in Loxo.
  2. Their work history, notes and resume are collected from Loxo.
  3. Claude reads them against that job's scorecard and writes a score per criterion, an average, a recommendation and a short assessment.
  4. The result is saved on the candidate in Loxo, marked as waiting for a recruiter's review.

What we need from you

Two things to run it on your real account. Everything else we set up.

  1. A Loxo API keyRead-only to start. Please do not send it by email or chat; we will agree a safe way to hand it over.
  2. Which searches to testAll your open searches, or the ones you pick. We score every candidate on them, without writing anything to Loxo.

Ground rules

  • A recruiter reviews every score. The system gives a recommendation and its reasons; it never decides an outcome.
  • It starts read-only. Nothing is written to Loxo until you have seen a full trial run on your searches and approved it.
  • Candidate data stays in Loxo and a private project folder. It is never pasted into chats.
  • Before anything is promised, we test what your Loxo account actually provides, such as full resume text and a signal when a candidate is added.
