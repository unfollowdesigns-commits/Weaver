How it works

One pipeline, from your data to a decision

Your candidates never leave your own systems except to be read by Claude, under your firm's own account.

Your LoxoCandidatesWork history, resume text and notes, on the searches you choose.
WeaverPreparesRemoves names, contact details and pay. Keeps only what matters for the role.
ClaudeScoresReads each candidate against that search's scorecard and explains every point.
WeaverChecks and ranksRejects incomplete answers, computes the average and the rank.
Your LoxoScorecard on the cardScore, strongest signal, watch item and where to probe.
Your teamDecidesReviews the score, agrees or not, moves candidates on.
Your Claude, connected to LoxoAnswers on demandShortlists, call prep, client updates and database searches, using the same data and the scores Weaver wrote.

Your data stays yours: it runs in accounts your firm owns, your candidates' details are not used to train AI models, and you can switch any part off at any time.

The scorecard

Your scorecard, on every search

You already score candidates on nine criteria written for each search, 1 to 5, critically. Weaver works the same way. For each search it drafts the nine criteria from the job description; you approve or change them in a minute; from then on every candidate gets a first read in those terms.

Weaver · first readBefore anyone callsFrom the Loxo record and the resume, the minute a candidate is added. Tells your team who to call first and what to ask.
Your team · your screenAfter your screensYour own scorecard, with what only a conversation shows. Still yours, still what your clients see.
