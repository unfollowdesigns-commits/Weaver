Candidate scoring in Loxo

When candidates are added to a job, each one is read against your scorecard and gets a score and a short written assessment in Loxo. Your team reviews every one. This page shows what that would look like, and what we need from you to build it.

How it works

  1. A candidate is added to a job in Loxo.
  2. The system collects that candidate's details from Loxo.
  3. Claude reads them against the job's scorecard and writes the scores and a short assessment.
  4. The result is saved on the candidate in Loxo, marked as waiting for your review.

It can also score a whole job at once, for the 20 to 100 candidates you want to compare.

What we need from you

Five things. Send them in any order.

  1. A Loxo API keyRead-only to start. In Loxo it is created under the account's API or integrations settings. Please do not paste it in an email or chat. We will agree a safe way to hand it over.
  2. One job that has real candidates on itOpen the job in Loxo and send us the number at the end of the web address.
  3. Your scorecardThe list of criteria, the scale, and whether the criteria stay the same for every job or change per job.
  4. Where the score should go in LoxoA scorecard, a custom field, or a note on the candidate.
  5. Your Loxo planFound under account or billing settings. This needs Professional or above.

Ground rules

  • A person at your firm reviews every score. The system gives a recommendation with its reasons. It never decides an outcome.
  • It starts read-only. Nothing is written to Loxo until you have seen a trial run on one job and said yes.
  • Candidate data stays in Loxo and in a private project folder. It is not copied into chats.
  • We confirm what Loxo actually provides, such as resume text and how it signals a new candidate, before promising anything built on it. That is the first thing we test.
